## B. The header (your main concern) — confirmed cluttered
Verified on the live desktop header:
1. **Eight equal-weight links** (Home, Pricing, Gallery, Blog, About, Service Areas, FAQ, Contact) in small mono uppercase, spread edge to edge. No grouping, no hierarchy, nothing tells the eye where to go. This is the clutter you noticed.
2. **No primary call to action in the desktop header.** There is no "Get a free quote" button and no phone number in the bar. For a lead-gen cleaning site, the single most important action is missing from the top of every page. (Both exist only in the mobile drawer.)
3. Because the bar stretches full width, the link cluster floats far to the right, visually disconnected from the logo.

**Recommended direction:** cut the top nav to 4-5 real items and add a solid "Get a free quote" button (plus a click-to-call phone). Move the secondary items (FAQ, Service Areas, Blog, Gallery) into a "Services"/"More" grouping or rely on the footer. Two viable structures:
- **Option A (simplest):** `Services (dropdown) · Pricing · Gallery · About` + **[Get a free quote]** + phone. FAQ/Service Areas/Blog/Contact live in the footer.
- **Option B (services-forward):** a Services mega-item listing Window/House/Deep, then `Pricing · About · Contact` + **[Get a free quote]**.
This is a design decision for you; both fix the clutter and add the missing CTA.

## C. The admin panel (your other concern)
The dashboard itself is reasonably organised (left sidebar, 7 sections: Overview, Reviews, Leads, Pricing, Gallery, Blog, Site content, with unread badges). The problems are around it:
1. **It renders inside the full public marketing header and footer.** The floating site nav and the giant "New Green" footer wordmark wrap the admin, and the layout compensates with a large top padding hack. It does not read like an owner tool; it reads like a public page with a form on it.
2. **Login has no rate limiting and no lockout.** Five wrong passwords returned five instant 401s. A single shared password is the only gate.
3. **No Cloudflare Access at the edge.** `/admin` returns 200 to anyone (only the app password stands between the public and the dashboard). The project docs themselves called edge Access "URGENT / required" and it is not in place.
4. Single shared password, no individual accounts, no audit trail. Fine for one owner, but there is no "who changed what".

**Fix priority:** add login rate-limiting/lockout, put Cloudflare Access (free) in front of `/admin` and `/api/admin/*`, and give the admin its own minimal chrome (hide the marketing header/footer on `/admin`).

## D. Trust & content integrity (highest business risk)
1. **The 6 homepage reviews are fabricated fallback demos with stock-portrait avatars.** Live CMS reviews are empty (`/api/reviews` = `[]`), so the site is serving the built-in placeholder testimonials (Sarah Mitchell, Daniel Okafor, etc.) as if real.
2. **The homepage emits `aggregateRating` structured data (4.8 stars, 6 reviews) built from those fake testimonials.** Marking up fabricated reviews as real is against Google's review-snippet policy and can trigger a manual action. This is the single riskiest item on the site. **Either load real reviews and keep the schema, or remove the fake reviews and the aggregateRating markup until real ones exist.**
3. **"Owner-operated, and cleaning homes ... for over fifteen years."** Your own build notes put the real history at about 5 years. Verify and correct; an inflated tenure claim is both a trust and a legal-advertising risk.
4. **The Gallery is a fake before/after.** Live gallery data is empty, so the page shows six stock photos where the same image is used on both sides of the slider (a CSS filter fakes the "before"), under the heading "Real homes, real results ... the results we hand back." It is a prominent top-nav item pointing at staged stock. Either populate it with real job photos or remove it from the nav until it has real content.

## E. Security & abuse
1. **No security headers at all.** No HSTS, no Content-Security-Policy, no X-Frame-Options, no X-Content-Type-Options, no Referrer-Policy, no Permissions-Policy. The site collects names, emails, phones, and addresses, so at minimum add HSTS and clickjacking protection.
2. **Public form endpoints are open to spam.** `/api/quotes`, `/api/messages`, and `/api/reviews` allow CORS from any origin (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`) with no CAPTCHA, honeypot, or rate limit. Any bot can flood the owner's Leads inbox and the review queue. Add a honeypot + rate limit (Cloudflare Turnstile is free).
3. Admin API gating itself is OK now (the CMS `/api/admin/*` returns 401 without the secret, and the site proxy requires the login cookie). The weak points are the login (item C2) and the missing edge Access (C3). Make sure `CMS_ADMIN_SECRET` is a strong random value: it doubles as the session-cookie signing key.

## F. Performance
1. **The hero ships a 2600px remote image, unoptimized, to every device including phones.** `next/image` optimization is turned off (`unoptimized: true`), so a 375px phone downloads the full 2600px Unsplash JPEG. Combined with a `framer-motion` fade that starts at opacity 0, the first paint is a muddy gray with barely-legible text until the big image arrives. That hurts LCP, most on mobile. **Fix: self-host a properly sized hero (or turn image optimization on) and drop the opacity-0 start on the LCP text.**
2. **Two always-on WebGL canvases** (full-page water background + header water wordmark) run on every page. They are GPU-cheap but they are the reason the page is slow to composite. Confirm they are paused off-screen/on hidden tabs and are truly reduced-motion safe.

## G. Photography & assets
- The hero, before/after, service-area, and several other images are **hotlinked from Unsplash at runtime**. That is fragile (rate limits, image can be pulled, not owned, generic) and the hero is a stock architectural mansion, not a cleaning photo. Self-host real (or licensed) imagery. The build notes already flag "replace stock with New Green's own team photos before launch."

## H. SEO / technical
1. **`www` does not resolve** (NXDOMAIN). The apex works, but anyone typing `www.` gets a dead site. Either add the `www` custom domain (redirect to apex) or accept apex-only and make sure nothing links to `www`.
2. **robots.txt (Cloudflare-managed) blocks AI crawlers** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider, etc.). If you want visibility in AI answers / AEO (which you usually care about), reconsider that block.
3. Sitemap, canonicals, OpenGraph image, GA4 tag, and the `LocalBusiness`/`HomeAndConstructionBusiness` JSON-LD are all present and correct (aside from the fake `aggregateRating` in D2). No local `postal address` / geo in the JSON-LD though: for local SEO, add full NAP (address, geo, areaServed cities, opening hours) to the structured data.
